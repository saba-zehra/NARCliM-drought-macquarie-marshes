# NARCliM-drought-macquarie-marshes
What Sentinel-1 shows for 2017–2019 and what NARCliM projects for the future?
The Macquarie Marshes depend on river flow, and their wet area changes strongly between dry and wet years. We ask how often the dry and wet conditions seen in the past may occur in the future. First, we use Sentinel-1 radar images from 2017 to 2019 to map dry conditions in the marshes. We compare this with river gauge flow and with a drought index built from rainfall and evaporation. Second, we use NARCliM2.0 regional climate projections to count dry spells and wet pulses in the catchment for [low, medium and high] emissions scenarios. This does not predict future flooding directly, because river flow also depends on dam releases and water rules. It shows how often drought-like conditions may return, and what they did to the wetland in the past.
